Common questions

How do I restore my purchase?
Open the app, go to Settings and tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought with.

How do I cancel my subscription?
On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, tap Subscriptions and choose this app. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period to avoid being charged for the next one.

Are these the real test questions?
No. The questions in the app are original practice questions. The real test questions are not published. You are tested on the official handbook, Life in the United Kingdom: A Guide for New Residents.

Where do I book the real test?
Only through the official service on GOV.UK. You cannot book it in this app. Check GOV.UK for the latest rules before you book.

Please email us with the question text so that we can check and correct it.
