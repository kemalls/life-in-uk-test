SettleReady Privacy Policy

Effective date: 5 November 2026

SettleReady ("the app", "we") helps you prepare for the Life in the UK Test. It works on your device and we do not collect your personal data. This policy explains what the app handles, why, and the choices you have.

Summary

  • You do not need an account. We do not ask for your name, email address or phone number.
  • Your study progress and settings stay on your device. We do not receive them.
  • The app has no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and we do not sell your data.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple.

Information stored on your device

The app keeps the following only on your iPhone:

  • Your study progress: your answers, your mistakes and your favourite questions.
  • Your settings, such as dark mode.

This information may be included in your device backups, such as iCloud Backup, according to your own device settings. You can erase your progress and favourites at any time in the app under Settings > Clear data. Deleting the app deletes this information from your device.

Purchases and subscriptions

Payments and subscriptions are processed by Apple under your Apple Account. We never see or store your payment card or Apple Account details. Apple gives the app the status of your subscription so it can unlock Pro features. Apple provides developers with sales reports that do not identify you personally. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

Audio lessons

Audio lessons are read aloud by your device's built-in text-to-speech voice. The app does not use your microphone and does not record audio.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules a daily study reminder on your device. It is a local notification created on your iPhone, and we do not use a server to send it. You can turn it off at any time in your device's Settings.

What we do not do

  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No tracking as defined by Apple's App Tracking Transparency.
  • No third-party analytics or crash reporting tools. If you choose to share diagnostics with app developers in your device settings, Apple provides them to us in a form that does not identify you.
  • No location tracking.
  • No sale or sharing of personal information.

If you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to write. We use it only to reply to you, on the basis of our legitimate interest in supporting users, and we delete it when it is no longer needed. Please do not send us immigration documents or other sensitive information.

Children

The app is meant for adults preparing for the Life in the UK Test and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Because the app does not send us your study data, we do not hold it and cannot access it. If you have contacted us by email, you can ask us to access, correct or delete that correspondence. If you are in the UK or the EU, you also have the right to complain to your data protection regulator. In the UK this is the Information Commissioner's Office.

Not affiliated with the government

SettleReady is an independent study app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to the Home Office, UK Visas and Immigration, the Life in the UK Test provider or the UK government.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this page and its effective date.
